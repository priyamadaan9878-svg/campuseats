# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a web service for managing campus food and meal-related services. It helps users find food services, view available meals, and perform food-related tasks through the system.

## Who

- Students — use the system to find and access campus food services.
- Food service providers — manage food items and meal-related information.
- Administrators — manage users, services, and the overall system.
