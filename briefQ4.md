# CampusEats System Brief

## 1. System Overview (What It Does)
**CampusEats** is an integrated, multi-tenant digital dining and food delivery platform designed specifically for college campuses. It standardizes and streamlines campus dining operations by aggregating university cafeterias, local eateries, food trucks, and student delivery staff into a single cohesive ecosystem. The platform enables users to browse menus, place pre-orders or live delivery orders, process digital payments, track order status in real time, and manage pickup or desktop/dorm room delivery across campus zones.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Roles (Who Uses It)

* **Students, Faculty & Staff (Customers):** Primary end-users who browse dining options, customize orders, schedule pickups or campus deliveries, make payments, and leave reviews.
* **Campus Vendors & Kitchen Staff (Merchants):** Food hall vendors, university dining halls, and external campus-adjacent restaurants that manage menu items, receive incoming orders, track inventory availability, and update order prep status.
* **Couriers / Student Runners (Delivery Personnel):** On-campus couriers (often students) who accept delivery dispatch requests, pick up orders from vendors, navigate campus buildings, and complete door-to-dorm deliveries.
* **Campus System Administrators (Platform Owners):** University administrators who oversee vendor onboarding, manage campus boundary geofencing, regulate delivery protocols, enforce safety standard operating procedures (SOPs), and audit financial transactions.

---

## 3. Explicit Nouns (Entities, Objects & Services)

### Physical & Organizational Entities
* **Campus / Zone:** Geofenced university boundaries and defined delivery zones (e.g., North Quad, Library Plaza, Residence Halls).
* **Vendor / Eatery:** Dining halls, cafes, food trucks, and local partner restaurants operating on or near campus.
* **Customer Profile:** The account entity holding university ID credentials, contact info, dietary preferences, and payment methods.
* **Courier:** On-campus delivery agent profile linked to verification, availability status, and rating.

### Operational & Domain Objects
* **Menu Item:** Specific food or beverage item including description, price, nutritional data, and allergy tags.
* **Customization / Modifier Option:** Add-ons, portion adjustments, and special instructions attached to a menu item (e.g., extra sauce, dairy alternative).
* **Cart:** Temporary container holding selected items, calculated subtotal, taxes, service fees, and delivery tips before checkout.
* **Order:** Formalized transactional record containing order ID, timestamp, itemized list, fulfillment type (pickup vs. delivery), status, and pricing.
* **Payment / Transaction:** Financial ledger record processing university meal plan credits, credit/debit cards, or digital wallets.
* **Delivery Request / Dispatch:** Active job ticket detailing pickup origin, drop-off location (building/room number), courier assignment, and route tracking.
* **Review / Rating:** Numerical score and qualitative feedback left by customers for vendors or couriers.

---

## 4. Explicit Verbs (Actions, Tasks & Contracts)

### Customer Actions
* **Browse / Search:** Filter vendors and menu items based on cuisine type, prep time, dietary constraints, or campus location.
* **Customize:** Select modifiers, portion choices, and dietary specifications for menu items.
* **Assemble Cart:** Add, update quantities of, or remove items in the shopping cart.
* **Checkout / Submit Order:** Enter into a binding fulfillment agreement by committing items, selecting pickup/delivery time, and applying payment.
* **Pay / Authorize:** Validate and transfer funds via meal credits, payment gateways, or campus card integrations.
* **Track:** Monitor real-time status updates (Received $\rightarrow$ In Prep $\rightarrow$ Ready for Pickup / Out for Delivery $\rightarrow$ Delivered).
* **Rate & Review:** Provide post-fulfillment ratings and written feedback for food quality or delivery promptness.

### Vendor & Operations Actions
* **Onboard / Register:** Register dining establishments and establish operational contracts with the platform.
* **Manage Menu:** Publish, edit, enable/disable, or temporarily mark menu items as "out of stock."
* **Accept / Reject Order:** Acknowledge incoming orders or reject them during peak overflow/closure.
* **Prepare / Update Status:** Signal transitions in the order lifecycle (e.g., mark as "In Kitchen" or "Ready for Courier").

### Courier & Delivery Actions
* **Claim / Accept Dispatch:** Opt-in to accept an available delivery task within the courier app.
* **Pick Up:** Verify item accuracy with the vendor and take possession of the order.
* **Navigate / Transit:** Move across campus to the specified delivery location following campus-safe pedestrian or bike paths.
* **Deliver / Fulfill:** Hand off the package to the customer or place it at a designated drop point, confirming completion with photo or PIN verification.

### System & Administrative Contracts
* **Verify Student ID / Credentials:** Authenticate student status and meal plan balance against university database systems.
* **Route Dispatch:** Automatically assign delivery jobs to optimal couriers based on proximity and workload.
* **Reconcile / Disburse Payments:** Calculate net payout to vendors and delivery compensation to couriers after platform service fees.
